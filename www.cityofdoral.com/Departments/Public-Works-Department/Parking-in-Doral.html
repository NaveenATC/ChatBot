1. About Parking Program

City of Doral Parking Program

The City of Doral operates a managed parking program within designated areas to balance the needs of residents, businesses, employees, and visitors. The program is designed to ensure that parking spaces are used efficiently and fairly, while supporting the City’s economic vitality and quality of life.

Through time limits, paid parking and enforcement, the program promotes regular turnover of parking spaces so customers can more easily access businesses and services. These measures help reduce congestion, improve traffic circulation, and enhance overall safety for pedestrians, cyclists, and motorists.

The parking program also plays an important role in protecting residential neighborhoods by discouraging long-term, commuter, and unauthorized commercial vehicle parking in areas not intended for that use. By managing demand and encouraging compliance with posted regulations, the City helps maintain orderly streets and preserves neighborhood character.

Parking policies and locations are regularly evaluated to respond to changing conditions, special events, and community needs. Revenue generated through the program is reinvested into parking operations, mobility improvements, signage, technology, and customer service to continually improve the parking experience in Doral.

Where Parking Is Managed

Parking regulations are currently in effect in the following areas:

  • Downtown Doral (NW 54 St to south of NW 52 St; NW 79 Ave to NW 87 Ave)

 

  • Legacy Park Area (NW 82 St and NW 112 Ave) 

 

  • Doral Academy Middle/High School Area (NW 109 Ave)

 

  • NW 36 Street Corridor (between NW 104 Ave and NW 107 Ave)

 

  • Industrial Area (South of CityPlace) (NW 30 Terrace)

View more information on parking locations, rates, and time limits on the PayByPhone and/or ParkMobile apps.

How to Pay

On-street parking is paid through PayByPhone or ParkMobile.
Download the app at www.paybyphone.com or www.parkmobile.com and follow instructions on posted signage.

Resident Discount

City of Doral residents may qualify for a discounted on-street parking rate when enrolled in PayByPhone/ParkMobile. Discounts are only valid when parking at City of Doral managed on-street spaces.

To register residents must provide the PayByPhone/ParkMobile account number, mobile phone number, license plate, and upload a copy of the vehicle registration of each vehicle on the account. Vehicles must be registered in the City of Doral. Please note that this discount is valid for City of Doral residents only and enrollment in the program must be renewed annually.

To submit a residential discount application, click here.
To address questions and concerns please contact:
parkdoral@miamiparking.com | (305) 938-0072

Enforcement & Citations

Parking enforcement is operated in partnership with the Miami Parking Authority (MPA).
Citation payments and disputes are handled by the Miami-Dade County Parking Violations Bureau.
To review the status of a parking citation, click here.
MPA enforcement officers focus on education and compliance and do not have citation quotas.

Towing

Towing in public areas is solely administered by Doral Police. If you believe your vehicle was towed, contact Doral Police (Non-Emergency) at (305) 593-6699.

2. Programa de Estacionamiento

La Ciudad de Doral opera un programa de estacionamiento administrado dentro de áreas designadas para equilibrar las necesidades de los residentes, negocios, empleados y visitantes. El programa está diseñado para garantizar que los espacios de estacionamiento se utilicen de manera eficiente y equitativa, a la vez apoya la vitalidad económica y la calidad de vida de la Ciudad.

A través de límites de tiempo, estacionamiento de pago y control, el programa promueve la rotación regular de los espacios para que los clientes puedan acceder con mayor facilidad a los negocios y servicios. Estas medidas ayudan a reducir la congestión, mejorar la circulación vehicular y aumentar la seguridad general de peatones, ciclistas y conductores.

El programa de estacionamiento también cumple un papel importante en la protección de los vecindarios residenciales al desalentar el estacionamiento prolongado, de tipo “commuter”, y de vehículos comerciales no autorizados en áreas no destinadas para ese uso. Al gestionar la demanda y fomentar el cumplimiento de las regulaciones publicadas, la Ciudad contribuye a mantener calles ordenadas y a preservar el carácter de los vecindarios.

Las políticas y ubicaciones de estacionamiento se evalúan periódicamente para responder a condiciones cambiantes, eventos especiales y necesidades de la comunidad. Los ingresos generados por el programa se reinvierten en operaciones de estacionamiento, mejoras de movilidad, señalización, tecnología y servicio al cliente para mejorar continuamente la experiencia de estacionamiento en Doral.

Dónde se Administra el Estacionamiento

Actualmente, las regulaciones de estacionamiento están vigentes en las siguientes áreas:

  • Downtown Doral (NW 54 St hasta el sur de NW 52 St; NW 79 Ave hasta NW 87 Ave)

 

  • Área de Legacy Park (NW 82 St y NW 112 Ave)

 

  • Área de Doral Academy Middle/High School (NW 109 Ave)

 

  • Corredor de NW 36 Street (entre NW 104 Ave y NW 107 Ave)

 

  • Área Industrial (al sur de CityPlace) (NW 30 Terrace)

Para más información sobre ubicaciones de estacionamiento, tarifas y límites de tiempo, consulte las aplicaciones PayByPhone y/o ParkMobile.

Cómo Pagar

El estacionamiento en la vía pública se paga a través de PayByPhone o ParkMobile.
Descargue la aplicación en www.paybyphone.com o www.parkmobile.com y siga las instrucciones indicadas en la señalización.

Descuento para Residentes

Los residentes de la Ciudad de Doral pueden calificar para una tarifa con descuento en el estacionamiento en la vía pública al inscribirse en PayByPhone/ParkMobile ($1.50 por hora en lugar de la tarifa estándar de $3.00 por hora). Los descuentos solo son válidos al estacionar en espacios en la vía pública administrados por la Ciudad de Doral.

Para registrarse, los residentes deben proporcionar el número de cuenta de PayByPhone/ParkMobile, número de teléfono móvil, placa del vehículo y cargar una copia del registro del vehículo de cada vehículo en la cuenta. Los vehículos deben estar registrados en la Ciudad de Doral. Tenga en cuenta que este descuento es válido únicamente para residentes de la Ciudad de Doral y la inscripción en el programa debe renovarse anualmente.

Para enviar una solicitud de descuento residencial, haz clic aquí.
Preguntas e inquietudes, comuníquese con:
parkdoral@miamiparking.com | (305) 938-0072

Control y Multas

La administracion de estacionamiento se realiza en asociación con Miami Parking Authority (MPA). Los pagos y las disputas de multas son gestionados por Miami-Dade County Parking Violations Bureau.

Para revisar el estado de una multa de estacionamiento, haz clic aquí.

Los oficiales de MPA se enfocan en la educación y el cumplimiento, y no tienen cuotas de multas.

Remolque

Remolques en areas publicas de la ciudad son administrado por la Policía del Doral. Si cree que su vehículo fue remolcado, comuníquese con la Policía del Doral (Línea No-Emergente) al (305) 593-6699.

3. Pay for Parking with PayByPhone or ParkMobile in the City of Doral!

The City of Doral works with PayByPhone and ParkMobile to let you pay for vehicle parking in seconds at designated parking locations. Both apps provide a complete payment solution, send reminders when your parking session is about to expire, and allow you to extend your parking time remotely. Paying by app is convenient, easy, and secure.

How to Pay Using PayByPhone

1. Download the PayByPhone app from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Create an account or log in.
3. Enter the location shown on nearby parking signs.
4. Enter your license plate number.
5. Select your parking duration and confirm payment.

Click the buttons below to download the iOS or Android app for PayByPhone.

How to Pay Using ParkMobile

1. Download the ParkMobile app from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Create an account or log in.
3. Enter the zone number posted on parking signage (or select the location on the map).
4. Enter your license plate number.
5. Choose the amount of time you want to park.

Click the buttons below to download the iOS or Android app for ParkMobile.

4. ¡Pague el Estacionamiento con PayByPhone o ParkMobile en la Ciudad de Doral!

La Ciudad de Doral trabaja con PayByPhone y ParkMobile para que pueda pagar el estacionamiento de su vehículo en segundos en las ubicaciones de estacionamiento designadas. Ambas aplicaciones ofrecen una solución completamente sin efectivo, envían recordatorios cuando su sesión de estacionamiento está por vencer y le permiten extender su tiempo de estacionamiento de forma remota. Pagar mediante la aplicación es conveniente, fácil y Seguro.

Cómo Pagar con PayByPhone

1. Descargue la aplicación PayByPhone desde el App Store o Google Play.
2. Cree una cuenta o inicie sesión.
3. Ingrese el número de ubicación que aparece en la señalización de estacionamiento cercana.
4. Ingrese el número de placa de su vehículo.
5. Seleccione la duración de su estacionamiento y confirme el pago.

Haga clic en los botones a continuación para descargar la aplicación iOS o Android de PayByPhone.

Cómo Pagar con ParkMobile

1. Descargue la aplicación ParkMobile desde el App Store o Google Play.
2. Cree una cuenta o inicie sesión.
3. Ingrese el número de zona indicado en la señalización de estacionamiento (o seleccione la ubicación en el mapa).
4. Ingrese el número de placa de su vehículo.
5. Elija la cantidad de tiempo que desea estacionar.
6. Confirme y comience su sesión de estacionamiento.

Haga clic en los botones a continuación para descargar la aplicación iOS o Android de ParkMobile.

5. Frequently Asked Questions

City of Doral residents will receive a discount on on-street Pay by Phone / ParkMobile parking sessions (not valid with other discount programs). To register please provide the Pay By phone / ParkMobile account number, mobile phone number, license plate, and upload a copy of the vehicle registration of each vehicle on the account. Vehicles must be registered in the City of Doral. Click Here To Apply

Please note that this discount is valid for City of Doral residents only and enrollment in the program must be renewed annually.

Please email parkdoral@miamiparking.com or 305-938-0072 call with any questions or concerns.

How can I register for PayByPhone / ParkMobile?

It’s easy. To register, download the PayByPhone / ParkMobile app from the App Store or Google Play or visit their respective website at paybyphone.com / Parkmobile.com. Make sure you have your mobile phone number, email address, and credit card that will be used to pay for parking ready to complete your registration.

Once I find a parking space, how do I start my parking session?

Once registered, you only need to park, click on the PayByPhone / Parkmobile app on your mobile device, as your account information is already stored, enter the location number, select how long you wish to park for, confirm and you will be done! PayByPhone / Parkmobile is easy, convenient and will relieve you of the stress of having to account for the parking time to avoid a ticket.

What kind of mobile phone can I to pay?

Any phone. On smart phones, by going to the PayByPhone / Parkmobile website or downloading the mobile app. Regular phones can call PayByPhone at 888-680-7275 or ParkMobile 877-727-9978 to activate their session.

How can I avoid getting a parking ticket?

Both apps have a feature that will alert you via text message when your parking session is about to expire (check account setting to ensure this feature is activated), thus allowing you to extend the time without having to walk to the vehicle and avoid getting a citation.

How much does it cost to register or create an account?

Registration is free!

Do parking sessions require a minimum of minutes or hours?

Customers have the ability to add the number of minutes or hours up to a maximum of three hours consecutively in a particular location. Additional information will be displayed on the app before you complete the payment!

Are the PayByPhone rates the same throughout the city of Doral?

No, rates may vary by location number.

Can I use my PayByPhone / ParkMobile account outside of the limits of the City of Doral?

Yes, you can use your account at any city or location that offers the service. Simply look for the PayByPhone or ParkMobile logos and use that location/zone number. Within the South Florida region PayByPhone and ParkMobile are available in a variety of locations including Doral, Miami, Coral Gables, Miami-Dade County Parks and South Miami. This makes it easy for customers in these four municipalities to use the same system to pay for parking.

How do I extend the parking session using my Smartphone?

You can simply extend the time by clicking on the “extend” feature on the PayByPhone mobile app or by calling 1-866-234-7275 (PARK) before the parking session has expired. For ParkMobile, text ‘Park’ to 772233.

How do the parking enforcement officers know that I have paid for parking?

When you pay for parking, your parking status will be displayed on the handheld device used by the enforcement officers. Your payment will be verified through your license plate, please ensure it is correctly typed.

Can we add other vehicles to the same account?

Yes, for more details, click on “my account” in order to add more vehicles to your account.

How can we know that the credit card transaction is secured?

It is secured because the credit card number is encrypted when you sign up and it is never entered, displayed or spoken during a telephone transaction.

How do I know if PayByPhone/ParkMobile is available where I parked?

Parking signage will be well displayed in all of the locations where City of Doral parking is available. The signs will feature the City of Doral name, location number and steps to follow.

Can I use my PaybyPhone / ParkMobile account in the parking lots?

Currently, PaybyPhone / ParkMobile is only used for on-street parking in the city for Doral. If a parking lot offers the service with these vendors, they are privately owned and NOT City of Doral spaces. For other payment options in off-street parking lots and garages, we recommend you visit www.downtowndoral.com/parking/ or call 305-592-0681 for more information.

Can I obtain a payment receipt?

Yes, to print a receipt, please go to the open your parking application. Once you have logged in, the system will show you the account history where all of your transactions are saved. You will need to select the date and print the receipt.

Can I know in advance how much money will the parking session cost?

The app provides a quote of the total cost based on what location number you’ve entered and the duration you wish to park for.

If I decide to move my vehicle to another space within the same general area during my parking session, do I need to pay again, or can I finish the parking session I had already paid for?

Yes, you can move your vehicle to another parking space as long as the location/zone number you used to pay for parking is the same.

Is it possible to receive a parking citation/ticket even though I paid?

Although unlikely, the MPA recommends you review the copy of your receipt to ensure the location number, license plate number, and time for which you paid matches the space where your vehicle was parked, license plate of your vehicle, and is within the time indicated on your citation. If you entered the right location and license plate number and your time had not expired, email parkdoral@miamiparking.com  to apply for a citation waiver.

 

If you believe that you were issued a citation in error, you may request that the citation be waived (cancelled).
You will be notified in via e-mail of the decision to waive (cancel) or not waive the citation.
Comments or concerns may be sent to parkdoral@miamiparking.com.

Please note that, under Florida law, e-mail addresses are public records. If you do not want your e-mail address released in response to a public records request, do not send electronic mail to this entity. Instead, contact this office by phone or in writing. See Sec. 668.6076, Florida Statutes.

How to Submit a Waiver Request

  • Fill out and submit the Waiver Request Form below.
  • Attach a copy of the citation.
  • Attach a copy of paid status support document (receipt, credit card statement, etc.)

Permit Guidelines

• Permit Type: Residential Permit
• Price: $40 per permit, per month
• Availability: Limited; valid only in approved permit zones
• Limitations: Permit quantities may be limited per household and/or per vehicle, in accordance with ordinance requirements

Eligibility Requirements

To apply, residents must submit the following required documents:
• Valid Driver’s License
• Current Vehicle Registration
Both documents must reflect a City of Doral residential address. Vehicles must be registered to a City of Doral address to qualify. In some instances, additional documents may be required to verify residence.

Vehicle Restrictions

Vehicles used for commercial purposes may not eligible for residential permits. This includes, but is not limited to:
• Marked or branded vehicles
• Work vans
• Company or fleet vehicles
• Vehicles registered to a business entity

Additional Information

Residential permits are non-transferable and will auto renew monthly. They do not guarantee parking availability. All vehicles must comply with posted signage, time limits, and zone-specific regulations at all times.

 

Commercial Parking Permits

The City of Doral offers a limited number of Commercial Parking Permits to support businesses operating within designated areas of the city. These permits are intended for employees and business owners who require long-term parking access in approved zones.
Commercial permits are issued on a monthly basis and are valid 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, unless otherwise restricted by posted signage or zone-specific regulations.

Permit Guidelines

• Permit Type: Commercial Parking Permit
• Price: $40 per permit, per month
• Availability: Limited; valid only in approved commercial permit zones

Eligibility Requirements

Applicants must submit the following required documentation:

• Proof of Business Ownership or Proof of Employment with a business located in the City of Doral

All documentation must be current and verifiable.

Vehicle Eligibility

Only vehicles registered to the permit holder are eligible. Vehicles must be associated with a qualifying business use.

Note: Commercial parking permits are specifically intended for vehicles used for commercial purposes. Personal vehicles not associated with a business may not qualify under this permit category.

Additional Information

Commercial permits are non-transferable, subject to availability, and do not guarantee parking availability. All vehicles must comply with posted signage, zone rules, and enforcement regulations at all times. Permit terms, eligible zones, and pricing may be modified as the program expands beyond Phase 1.

9. Downtown Doral South Residential Parking FAQ’s

This neighborhood-based parking system was developed in response to residents concerns about limited parking availability, long walking distances to available spaces, and insufficient guest parking options. After hosting a public workshop where residents were invited to share feedback, the City moved forward with implementing the program. In addition, the City met with the Community Development District (CDD) Board during two separate public meetings, where the program was discussed and supported as neighborhood representation.

Parking Hours

  • Complimentary Parking: 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM
  • Permit / Paid Parking Enforcement: 6:00 PM – 6:00 AM

During overnight hours, only permitted vehicles or paid sessions will be authorized to park within the regulated area.

Residential Permit Overview

  • Cost: $40 per month
  • Renewal: Auto-renewing monthly unless canceled
  • Limit: One (1) permit per household (current policy)
  • Availability: Parking is first-come, first-served
  • Important: A parking space is not guaranteed near the residence

Each household is entitled to one permit, and exceptions based on housing type or garage size would create inequities and complicate enforcement.

Two Permit Zones Established

To better manage parking demand and ensure neighborhood access remains balanced, the Downtown Doral South area has been divided into two residential permit zones.

Permits are only valid at registered zone; signage will display as such:

  • Zone 1 – North (NW 52nd Terr. – Central Park Blvd.)
  • Zone 2 – South (Central Park Blvd. – NW 41st St.)

Each residence is assigned to a specific zone based on its location. Residential permits are valid only within the boundaries of the assigned zone. This structure ensures that parking availability is preserved for residents within their immediate neighborhood and prevents displacement between areas.

Dividing the community into two zones allows the City to:

  • Better distribute limited parking inventory
  • Reduce long walking distances from available spaces
  • Prevent over-concentration of vehicles in one portion of the neighborhood
  • Maintain fairness and predictability in overnight parking

This zoning approach is a standard best practice in residential parking management and helps protect access for those who live within each defined area.

Eligibility Requirements for Permits

To obtain a residential permit, applicants must provide:

  • Government-issued ID with an eligible Doral address
  • Vehicle registration
  • Utility bill or proof of residency

Commercial vehicles are not eligible.

Overnight Parking Options for Non-Permit Holders

For visitors or residents needing additional parking spaces:

  • $1.50 per hour for Doral residents registered through PayByPhone or ParkMobile
  • $3.00 per hour for all other visitors

Payment is available through the PayByPhone or ParkMobile mobile applications.

To register, residents must provide the PayByPhone/ParkMobile account number, mobile phone number, license plate, and upload a copy of the vehicle registration of each vehicle on the account. Vehicles must be registered in the City of Doral. Please note that this discount is valid for City of Doral residents only and enrollment in the program must be renewed annually.

Please email parkdoral@miamiparking.com to apply or call the Miami Parking Authority at (305) 938-0072 if further assistance is required.

Why This Program Was Implemented

This initiative directly addresses:

  • Limited neighborhood parking inventory
  • Long walking distances from available spaces
  • Lack of structured guest parking management
  • The need to prioritize residents during overnight hours

By regulating overnight parking, the City aims to better manage parking supply, improve accessibility for residents, and create a more predictable and organized neighborhood parking system with the added benefit of offering a dedicated enforcement officer to consistently patrol the area which deters mischief, vandalism, and speeding.
