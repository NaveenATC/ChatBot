Morgan Levy Park

Morgan Levy Park is home to many wonderful amenities, including the very popular soccer fields, which have seen an estimated 60,000 residents on the fields alone during the course of the year. The original synthetic turf was installed 9 years ago, and throughout the years the field has required several repairs, and in order to maintain the highest quality and safety for our residents, the fields underwent a much needed makeover with the replacement of the synthetic turf.

*Indoor Facility Rental is now available on Saturdays as well as Sundays!

Maintenance Notice:
The tennis courts at Morgan Levy Park will be undergoing resurfacing over the next three weeks to improve playability and overall user experience. 
During this time, the courts will be temporarily closed. We appreciate your patience as we complete these important upgrades.
