Doral Meadow Park

Ideal for youth and adult sports leagues, this park has a little bit of something for everyone. Built in 2006, this park features the following amenities over 14 acres:

  • One (1) 30’ x 30’ pavilion available for rental
  • Shaded, lighted playground
  • Concession building with covered pavilion
  • Vita Course exercise path
  • Two (2) lighted soccer/football fields
  • Two (2) baseball fields
  • Fitness walking/jogging trail
  • Batting cages
  • Bikeway access to Trails & Tails Park

Construction Notice:
Please be advised that the picnic area and playground at Doral Meadow Park are currently under constructions as part of ongoing park improvements. These areas will be temporarily unavailable during this time. We appreciate your patience and understanding as we work to enhance the park experience for our community.

 

ATTENTION:
For the comfort and safety of our patrons, domestic animals, including canines, felines, and other household pets, are not permitted at this facility. The provisions of this rule shall not apply to the use of a special needs service animal. Please refer to Section 29-38 of the City of Doral Code of Ordinances for more information.
