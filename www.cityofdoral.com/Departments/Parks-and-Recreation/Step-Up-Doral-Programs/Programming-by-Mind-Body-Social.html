Programming by Mind Body Social

  2. Wellness Wednesdays by Mind Body Social

1. Overview

Mind Body Social offers a variety of wellness programs at various Doral Parks! Monthly Wellness Wednesday programming, bi-weekly Zumba, monthly VXN workouts and more can be found on the link below! Please be sure to follow the City of Doral’s social media pages for updates on upcoming dates and offerings!

2. Wellness Wednesdays by Mind Body Social

Join us each third Wednesday of the month for Wellness Wednesday! These classes are brought to the community for free as part of the Mayors Health Initiative, Step Up Your Health, Doral!

Dates

Dates On-going

Fees
FREE

Days and Times

Day(s): Third wednesday of the month
Time(s): 6:30pm - 7:30pm

Contact information

For more information, visit www.MindBodySocialEvents.com.

3. Downtown Doral Run Club

Baptist Health Doral Run Club meets every Monday at 7pm at Downtown Doral Park! Meet at the corner of 84th Avenue and NW 53 Terr, across from the Government Center. There are no fees involved. Just show up to join the group and run every week. The distance is between 3-3.5 miles.

Downtown Doral Park

8395 NW 53rd Street
