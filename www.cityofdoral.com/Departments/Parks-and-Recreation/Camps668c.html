1. Doral Central Park Summer Camp

Join us this summer for a fun-filled, action-packed camp right here at Doral Central Park! This camp is designed for kids ages 5–13 and offers a mix of sports, arts & crafts, STEM activities, cooking classes, and outdoor adventures. Each week has a new theme, keeping campers engaged, learning, and making friends in a safe, supervised environment. With access to our indoor facilities, outdoor fields, water park, and teaching kitchen, every day is full of exciting opportunities to explore, create, and play!

Location

Doral Central Park

2. Camp Unbeatables Summer Camp

A Multi-Sport, Recreational and Life Skills Academy Included". Build by experts in Sports & Life Coaching for kids and families.

Ages 5-14

Camp Unbeatables is a fun, dynamic summer experience where kids stay active, engaged, and excited every single day! Campers enjoy a variety of sports, water days, creative activities like art, music and dance, on-site experiences such as science and magic shows, and optional premium field trips that expand their world beyond camp. Each week introduces a new social-emotional learning theme, brought to life through hands-on experiences, games, and group dynamics that make learning natural and engaging.

All of this takes place within a high-quality youth program designed by Camp Unbeatables — a specialized company with over 15 years of experience and more than 20,000 children and families impacted.

6. YMCA Summer Camp

Make this Spring Break unforgettable at the Y! Join us at the YMCA of South Florida for Spring Outdoor Camp that’s all about adventuring in nature and embracing the great outdoors. We’re on a mission to nurture self-reliance while fostering character and leadership through an array of exciting activities.
