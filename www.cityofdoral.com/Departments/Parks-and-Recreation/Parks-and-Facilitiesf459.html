Parks and Facilities

The Dept. operates and maintains eleven parks with an abundance of amenities for every age and lifestyle including: a community center, lighted artificial sports fields, basketball courts, tennis courts, volleyball courts, bike-ways, jogging trails, exercise stations, batting cages, shaded playgrounds, dog friendly off leash dog areas (Trails & Tails & Doral White Course Park) and covered pavilions/picnic tables available for party rentals. The City offers clean, safe and secure facilities for adults and children to play and natural areas for everyone to enjoy.

Doral Aquatic Center

3005 NW 92nd Avenue, Doral, FL 33172

The Doral Central Park Aquatic Center features a 50-meter competition pool and training pool, offering swim programs, lessons, and events for all ages.

Tagged as: , Swimming Pools & Water Play

Doral Central Park

3005 NW 92nd Avenue, Doral, FL 33172

Doral Central Park is the City of Doral’s largest park, featuring a wellness campus, amphitheater, playgrounds, athletic fields, and year-round community events.

Tagged as: , Nature, Trails, Multi-use Facility

Doral Cultural Arts Center

8363 NW 53rd Street, Doral, FL 33166

At approximately 14,000 square feet, the Doral Cultural Arts Center is designed to be the hub of cultural and performing arts in the City, with a large art gallery space, a multi-purpose room, outdoor courtyards/garden spaces, and rooftop terrace. This state-of-the-art multiuse facility is located next to Downtown Doral Park.

Tagged as: , Nature

Doral Glades Park

7600 NW 98 Place, Doral, FL 33178

Doral Glades Park is conceived as a place in which the community can gather to enjoy nature in a safe environment that is representative of the beautiful City of Doral. Covering about 25 acres, this park is located on NW 74th Street between NW 97th Avenue and NW 107th Avenue.

Tagged as: , Nature, Sports Grounds, Trails

Doral Legacy Park

11400 NW 82 Street, Doral, FL 33178

Doral Legacy Park is conceived as a place in which the community can gather to enjoy sports, cultural arts, and nature in a safe environment that is representative of the beautiful City of Doral. The park will encompass 18+ acres in the section 7 area of Doral.

Tagged as: , Indoor Sports Centre, Multi-use Facility, Sports Complex

Doral Meadow Park

11555 NW 58th Street, Doral, FL 33178

Ideal for youth and adult sports leagues, this park has a little bit of something for everyone.

Tagged as: , Sports Grounds, Trails

Downtown Doral Park

8395 NW 53rd Street, Doral, FL 33166

Adjacent to the City of Doral Government Center, this park encompasses 3 acres in the heart of the booming Downtown Doral area. Completed in 2012, this passive park is ideal for family picnics or an afternoon stroll through its elegant walkways and lush landscaping.

Tagged as: , Passive Park

MAU Park

3719 NW 97th Avenue, Doral, FL 33178

This .68 acre park is located next to Millennia Atlantic University, thanks to a public-private partnership between the City and Millennia Atlantic University. Completed in 2019, this passive park is ideal for studying and picnics. It is located next to the Doral Police Training and Community Center.

Tagged as: , Public Private Partnership

Morgan Levy Park

5300 NW 102nd Avenue, Doral, FL 33178

Morgan Levy Park is home to many wonderful amenities, including the very popular soccer fields, which have seen an estimated 60,000 residents on the fields alone during the course of the year. The original synthetic turf was installed 9 years ago, and throughout the years the field has required several repairs, and in order to maintain the highest quality and safety for our residents.

Tagged as: , Sports Grounds, Trails, Multi-use Facility

Trails & Tails Park

11645 NW 50th Street, Doral, FL 33178

Completed in 2012, this eight acre facility is Doral’s first dog-friendly facility. Small and large dog off-leash areas offer residents the opportunity to play catch or run around with their four-legged best friends.

Tagged as: , Trails, Dog Park
