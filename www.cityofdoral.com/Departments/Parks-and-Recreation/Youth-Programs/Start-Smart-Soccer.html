Start Smart Soccer

Start Smart Soccer prepares children and their parents for organized soccer without the threat of competition or the fear of getting hurt. Age appropriate equipment is used in teaching kicking, dribbling, trapping, throw ins and agility. Parents are required to remain with their child for the duration of class for additional assistance.

AGES 3 - 5

Online Registration
