Police Department

Overview

The Police Department is committed to protecting life and property, preserving public safety, and maintaining order throughout the City of Doral. Through proactive policing, community partnerships, and professional service, the Department works to ensure a safe environment for residents, businesses, and visitors.

Officers and civilian staff are dedicated to upholding the law with integrity, transparency, and accountability while fostering trust within the community.

Services

The Police Department provides:

  • Patrol and emergency response services
  • Criminal investigations
  • Traffic enforcement and crash investigations
  • Community policing and outreach programs
  • Crime prevention and public safety education

Dial 911 for EMERGENCIES ONLY
