Parks & Recreation

Overview

The Parks & Recreation Department proudly offers award-winning programs and modern facilities that are open to the public seven days a week, including most holidays. With a strong commitment to community wellness and engagement, the Department provides recreational programs, cultural activities, and family-friendly events for residents and visitors of all ages.

Each year, more than a million people enjoy Doral’s parks, programs, and events — spaces designed to bring the community together and enhance quality of life throughout the City.

Services

The Parks & Recreation Department is responsible for:

  • Maintaining City parks and recreational facilities
  • Offering youth, adult, and senior programs
  • Hosting community and cultural events
  • Coordinating facility rentals and park reservations
  • Supporting health and wellness initiatives
