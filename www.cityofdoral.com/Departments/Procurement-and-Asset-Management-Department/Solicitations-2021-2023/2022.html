2022-22 ITB NW 58th Street Outfall

Description

The City of Doral desires to retain the services of a qualified and licensed Contractor, or a Certified Engineering Contractor to construct stormwater drainage improvements along NW 57th Street between NW 79th Avenue and NW 78th Avenue (Known as Phase I) and along NW 79th Avenue between NW 57th Street to NW 58th Street to construct along the NW 58th Street canal bank a 48” Outfall to discharge from the south bank of the NW 58th Street Canal (Known as Phase I). Roadway restoration and milling & resurfacing is required for this project. Also, canal bank improvements of pond 1 and pond 2 of NW 58th Street Canal to improve the top of bank of each canal segment to reach historical flood elevations. For a more detailed project overview refer to the attached construction plan set titled “Sub Basin H‐5 NW 58th Street Canal and Outfall Improvements.”
