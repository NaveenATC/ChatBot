Overview

The Building Department ensures safe and compliant construction in Doral. We manage permitting, inspections, and code compliance to protect residents, businesses, and visitors while supporting responsible development.

Responsibilities

  • Issue building permits and review construction plans
  • Conduct inspections for code compliance and safety
  • Enforce local building codes and ordinances
  • Guide contractors, designers, and property owners
  • Maintain permit and inspection records
  • Answer questions about construction requirements
