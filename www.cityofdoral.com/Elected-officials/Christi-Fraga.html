Dear Doral Residents,

It’s truly an honor to serve as your Mayor, and every day, I am inspired by the strength and resilience of our community. Doral isn’t just a city it’s a place where families come to build their lives, businesses thrive, and dreams become reality. Whether you’re raising your children, growing your business, or enjoying a vibrant lifestyle, Doral is where opportunity meets possibility.

Together with the City Council, we are dedicated to making Doral a brighter and more prosperous place to live, work, learn and play. We’ve built a city where faith, family, and freedom are the core of our values. These principles guide everything we do and are the reason Doral has become home to so many who seek a life full of opportunity and prosperity.

Our community is defined by a sense of belonging. It’s not just the beautiful parks or the bustling businesses—it’s the people. Whether you're enjoying a delicious arepa, sharing a BBQ with family at one of our parks, or savoring a dish from one of our many international restaurants, Doral is a celebration of cultural diversity. From Latin American flavors to global cuisines, our city's rich multicultural fabric is woven into every corner, making it a place where traditions, music, art, and stories from all over the world come together. We embrace these vibrant traditions, ensuring that Doral remains a welcoming home where everyone can find a sense of belonging and celebrate the uniqueness of our shared community.

As we look to the future, we’re committed to smart growth that fosters innovation, supports local businesses, and ensures our city remains a beacon of safety and opportunity. Our investments in public safety and infrastructure will continue to make Doral one of the most desirable places to live in Florida. We are a community that values hard work, integrity, and the opportunity for everyone to succeed.

I encourage you to stay involved in our city’s growth and development. Doral thrives because of the energy and engagement of its residents. Together, we will continue building a city where prosperity and the American dream are not just ideals, but a reality.

Let’s continue to create a Doral that we can all be proud of a place where families are safe, businesses are thriving, and everyone can enjoy the benefits of hard work and opportunity.

With gratitude and determination,


Christi Fraga
Mayor

Mayor Christi Fraga Biography

The City of Doral has been home to Christi Fraga and her Cuban-American family for over twenty years. When the time came for Christi and her husband, Gabriel Fraga, to decide where to raise their son Gabriel Alexander, Doral was the only choice. Her deep roots in this community have shaped her vision and commitment to creating a city where families can thrive.

Christi earned an associate degree in business administration from Miami-Dade Community College (MDCC) and a bachelor’s degree in financial management and accounting from Florida International University (FIU). In 2014, she also completed the Good Government Initiative’s Leaders in Excellence Program at the University of Miami, cultivating her ethical leadership skills.

Christi’s journey in public service began in 2012 when she became the youngest person in Doral's history to be elected to the City Council. She was later appointed Vice Mayor for three of the eight years she served. Her passion for empowering youth inspired her to establish the Youth Advisory Board, promoting civic leadership and engagement. Additionally, Christi championed the needs of women and senior citizens by leading initiatives such as the Women’s Alliance Group and advocating for resources that enhance quality of life.

Her leadership and dedication to education led her to serve on the Miami-Dade School Board, where she recommended improvements and voted on overseeing a $7 billion budget. She served on the Miami-Dade Value Adjustment Board, the School Board’s Facilities Committee, and the Audit and Budget Committee. During her tenure, she advocated for increased resources for special needs students, emphasized in importance of financial literacy, and promoted mental health initiatives.

In 2022, Christi was elected as Mayor of Doral, becoming the first woman to hold the position. In November 2024, she made history by being re-elected with 80% of the vote—the highest percentage in Doral’s history. Under her leadership, Doral has experienced transformative progress while maintaining fiscal responsibility and renewed trust in government.

Public safety has been a cornerstone of Christi’s results driven approach. Under her leadership and with the support and partnership of the City’s Chief of Police, crime in Doral has decreased by 30%. Key initiatives have included improvements to the Doral Connect program and the Doral license plate readers (LPR Program), enhancing public safety through innovative technology. She championed the launch of new specialized units like the HOA Fraud Unit and the Aggressive Driving Unit, addressing community-specific safety concerns. Her strong focus on community policing has fostered trust, transparency, and collaboration between residents and law enforcement, making Doral an even safer and more attractive place to live and open a business.

A visionary for smart, responsible, and sustainable growth, Christi has prioritized infrastructure and traffic solutions. Focused on redesigning the City’s trolley routes, making them more direct and efficient, reducing commute times, and increasing accessibility. Notably, she launched the Doral-FIU trolley route during her tenure as a councilmember, a direct and highly-utilized route recognized for its role in completing the “final mile” initiative, bridging Doral and FIU students and families.

Christi’s leadership has been defined by her relentless pursuit of transparency, government efficiency, and constituent engagement. She reestablished the Office of Constituent Services, ensuring residents have direct access to solutions and assistance. She also created InfoDoral, a one-stop resource center at City Hall for residents and business owners, cutting through bureaucracy and streamlining government processes. These initiatives have built new trust in government, fostering a culture of accessibility and innovation.

One of Christi’s most impactful accomplishments has been the completion of Doral Central Park. When she took office, the park had been closed for nearly three years. As promised, in less than 18 months, she delivered the first phases, and within three years, opened the entire 82 acer park featuring world class facility’s, an amphitheater, skate park, children’s playgrounds, an active lake, an aquatic facility, Olympic swimming pool and more—fulfilling the City’s $150 million bond commitment to residents. This achievement symbolizes her dedication to bringing real results to the community, enhancing the quality of life for all who live, work, learn, and play in Doral.

During her term Doral has achieved a historic Triple AAA bond rating, reflecting the City’s exceptional creditworthiness. This achievement translates to lower borrowing costs and greater financial flexibility, ensuring that taxpayer dollars are spent efficiently and responsibly. Christi also established Doral’s Repairs and Replacement Fund, strategically saving for the upkeep of City assets and proactively planning for fiscal impacts years in advance. Her administration has maintained the lowest millage rate in the county while preserving a healthy reserve at four times the required amount, ensuring the City’s long-term financial health.

Christi has also been a fierce advocate for relocating the Miami-Dade Incinerator out of Doral, championing environmental health and community well-being. Her focus on sustainability is matched by her commitment to responsible growth and smart development, ensuring that Doral continues to thrive while preserving its unique character.

As a visionary leader, Christi’s forward-thinking agenda includes leaving new revenue-generating assets for the City’s future, enhancing turn around times within the Building Department, and ensuring that businesses continue to thrive in Doral. She is also committed to enhancing Economic Development, introducing new business incentives, and establishing senior and special needs housing opportunities to support the community’s most vulnerable populations.

Christi Fraga’s unwavering dedication to Doral has earned her recognition from numerous organizations, including the prestigious Lifetime Achievement Award from the South Florida Hispanic Chamber of Commerce. Her leadership is defined by delivering real results, driving innovation, and building a vibrant and prosperous community for generations to come.

Christi Fraga’s vision is clear: to make Doral the best place to live, work, learn, and play. Guided by her passion, integrity, and commitment to service, she continues to lead Doral toward achieving a bright and bold future.

Mayor for a Day

Celebrating Leadership

The annual Mayor for a Day program is designed to inspire the next generation of local leaders by offering a unique, hands-on look at public service.

The selected student:

  • Spends the day with Mayor Fraga and City staff
  • Participates in meetings and activities
  • Learns about city operations, decision-making, and community impact

This experience aligns with Make a Dream Come True Day, celebrating opportunities that motivate and empower students to lead!
