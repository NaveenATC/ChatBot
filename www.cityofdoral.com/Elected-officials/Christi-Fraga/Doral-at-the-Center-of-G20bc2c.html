City of Doral

Doral at the Center of G20

The G20 Summit brings together leaders from the world’s major economies to address the most pressing global challenges, from economic growth and trade to innovation and sustainability. In 2026, this international gathering will take place at the Trump National Resort in Doral, placing our region at the center of global dialogue and decision-making.

Why Doral

Strategically located just minutes from Miami International Airport and home to a thriving international business community, Doral serves as a natural hub for global connectivity.

Its proximity, infrastructure, and reputation as a center for commerce and culture position the city as an ideal setting to host key gatherings surrounding the Summit.

Doral’s Role

In coordination with regional partners, the City of Doral will host a series of curated, high-level auxiliary events designed to welcome international delegations, facilitate meaningful dialogue, and showcase the vibrancy of our community. Building on the success of recent high-level international engagements, including the Shield of the Americas Summit Welcome Reception, the City is well-positioned to deliver events of this caliber. Additional details and the full event schedule will be announced soon.

Frequently Asked Questions Everything you need to know about the G20 in Doral
What is the G20?
The G20, or Group of Twenty, is an international forum of the world’s largest economies. Its members represent approximately 85% of global GDP, 75% of international trade, and two-thirds of the world’s population. Leaders meet annually to coordinate on economic policy, financial stability, and sustainable development.
When and where is the 2026 G20 Summit?
The 2026 G20 Leaders’ Summit will take place on December 14–15, 2026 at the Trump National Doral Miami in Doral, Florida. This marks the 21st annual G20 Summit and the first to be held in South Florida.
What events is the City of Doral hosting?
The City of Doral will host a series of auxiliary events surrounding the Summit, including a Welcome Reception for international delegations, a Business Connection Forum to foster economic partnerships, and Global Fest: Doral Where the World Meets—a community celebration showcasing Doral’s multicultural vibrancy. Dates and details will be announced soon.
Can the public attend G20 events?
The G20 Leaders’ Summit itself is a closed diplomatic event. However, the City of Doral’s auxiliary events—including Global Fest—are designed to engage residents and visitors. Details on public participation and registration will be shared as event plans are finalized.
How can my organization become a sponsor or partner?
A limited number of partnership opportunities are available for organizations looking to align their brand with this landmark international gathering. You can contact the sponsorship team at sponsorship@cityofdoral.com or download the sponsorship deck from this page for full details on packages and visibility benefits.
Which countries are part of the G20?
The G20 members are Argentina, Australia, Brazil, Canada, China, France, Germany, India, Indonesia, Italy, Japan, Mexico, Russia, Saudi Arabia, South Africa, South Korea, Türkiye, the United Kingdom, the United States, the European Union, and the African Union (a permanent member since 2023).
Will there be road closures or security impacts in Doral?
As with any event of this scale, temporary security measures and traffic adjustments may be in effect around the Summit venue and surrounding areas. The City of Doral will work closely with local, state, and federal agencies to minimize disruption. Residents and businesses will receive advance notice through Doral Alerts and official city channels.
How can I stay updated on G20 developments?
Sign up for the City of Doral newsletter and follow the city on social media for the latest announcements about events, sponsorship opportunities, and community updates as the Summit approaches.
