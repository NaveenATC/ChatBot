Economic Development Reports

Explore Doral’s economic growth and opportunities through our comprehensive collection of reports and data analyses. These reports provide essential insights into market trends, demographic shifts, industry sectors, and real estate development, equipping business owners, investors, city planners, and community members with the knowledge to make confident, informed decisions. Whether you are considering opening a business, expanding your operations, or simply want to stay updated on Doral’s evolving economy, these reports offer a wealth of valuable information.
Browse our latest reports below, and don’t hesitate to contact our Economic Development team if you need further assistance or customized data.

Biannual Report January - June 2025

The Economic Development division’s mission is to promote the City of Doral as a business-friendly destination for existing and new-to-market businesses interested in expanding their operations in the City of Doral. The goal of the Biannual Economic Reports is to provide businesses, investors, residents, and all interested stakeholders with an overview of Doral’s evolving economy, while showcasing economic programs designed to support the expansion of local businesses and attract new businesses.

Quarterly Report Q1 - Q2 2022

The Economic Development division promotes the City of Doral as a business-friendly destination and assists businesses interested in opening in, relocating to, or expanding their operations in, the City of Doral. The division builds relationships with business and government, administers grants, capitalizes on opportunities, and seeks out new businesses. The division offers orientation, economic data, site aid, referrals, and individualized assistance to new businesses. The division helps existing firms with information, education, access to capital, connections and promotions. The division generates targeted leads through advertising, media messaging, content, and promotions. Other outreach mediums include presentations and educational events.

Economic Analysis: Report I - Competitive Assessment

The Florida International University Metropolitan Center is Florida’s leading urban policy think tank and solutions center. Established in 1997, the Center provides economic development, strategic planning, community revitalization, and performance improvement services to public, private and non-profit organizations in South Florida. Its staff and senior researchers are leaders in their respective fields, and bring extensive research, practical, and professional experience to each project. The Center’s research has catalyzed major policy initiatives and projects in housing, economic redevelopment, transportation, social services, and health services throughout South Florida.
