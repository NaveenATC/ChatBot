Promote Your Business

We are looking for Doral businesses that want to create a positive impact on our economy and community by partnering with the City to encourage residents to Spend Local, Save Local.

Businesses with a valid Doral Business Tax Receipt are invited to participate by offering a unique deal or discount on products and/or services to Doral residents who present a Spend Local card.

Participation gives your business increased exposure while supporting our local economy and rewarding loyal residents!

For more details or to edit an existing account, please contact EconomicDevelopment@CityofDoral.com.
