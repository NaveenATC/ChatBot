Connect with Partners

At the City of Doral, we believe strong partnerships are the foundation of a thriving economic ecosystem. Our Economic Development Partners play a vital role in supporting local businesses, attracting investment, and fostering sustainable growth throughout the community. This collaborative network includes government agencies, business organizations, educational institutions, and regional economic development groups. Together, we work to provide resources, expertise, and opportunities that empower entrepreneurs and companies to succeed.

Explore our key partners below and discover how their services and programs complement Doral’s commitment to economic vitality.

How to Get Involved
Interested in partnering with the City of Doral or learning more about our network? Contact us to explore collaboration opportunities and access valuable resources.

Business Assistance Partnerships

According to author John Naisbitt, “The role of governments should be to create a flourishing environment for entrepreneurs and companies to flourish.” The Economic Development division works closely with many outstanding institutions that offer a wealth of valuable support services to the city’s businesses. Here are just a few of the trusted partners that have been aiding Doral’s firms to succeed.

Ascendus

Ascendus empowers low- to moderate-income (LMI) business owners with access to capital and financial education.

ascendus.org

 

Business Innovation & Technology Center at MDC

The BIT Center serves as a hub for exploring and cultivating the role of real-time technologies in digital transformation, process automation, and business analytics, offering students a real-world competitive advantage.
Business Innovation and Technology Center | Miami Dade College

mdc.edu

 

CareerSource South Florida

CareerSource South Florida assists employers and job seekers with employment services and trainings, providing business incentives and offering valuable resources to our diverse community through a network of centers throughout the region.

CareerSource Florida

 

Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services

The Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services supports and promotes Florida agriculture, protects the environment, safeguards consumers, and ensures the safety and wholesomeness of food.
Home - Florida Department of Agriculture & Consumer Services

fdacs.gov

 

Florida’s SBDC at FIU

Florida’s SBDC @ FIU brings local companies a team of highly experienced business experts who offer confidential, no-cost consulting to entrepreneurs and business owners looking to grow.
Florida International University

Florida SBDC

 

Florida State Minority Supplier Development Council (FSMSDC)

The mission of the FSMSDC is to link Corporate and Government Members with Minority Business Enterprises to foster equity in business.
FSMSDC - Connecting MBEs to More Opportunities

Florida State Minority Supplier Development Council

 

Goldman Sachs 10,000 Small Businesses

The Goldman Sachs 10,000 Small Businesses program helps entrepreneurs create jobs and economic opportunity by providing access to education, capital and support services.
Goldman Sachs 10,000 Small Businesses

10ksbapply.com

 

Greater Miami Convention and Visitors Bureau (GMCVB)

The Greater Miami Convention & Visitors Bureau is the official, accredited destination sales and marketing organization for Greater Miami and Miami Beach.
About the GMCVB | Greater Miami & Miami Beach

miamiandbeaches.com

 

HIAS Economic Advancement Fund

The HIAS Economic Advancement Fund manages a Small Business Program that assists forcibly displaced entrepreneurs and business owners from other countries that are looking to start or expand a business in the U.S. They provide coaching, micro loans and gig economy loans to help them achieve self-sufficiency.

hiaseaf.org/

 

Millennia Atlantic University (MAU)

Millennia Atlantic University (MAU) offers business-focused undergraduate and graduate programs with an emphasis on business administration, management, and entrepreneurship.

Millennia Atlantic University (MAU)

 

MDC WORKS

MDC WORKS Career Studio is a professional development resource giving MDC students the skills they need to successfully launch their careers and create meaningful futures.

MDC Works | Miami Dade College

 

Miami Bayside Foundation

Miami Bayside Foundation is a nonprofit organization designed to advance economic development in South Florida through the support of minority businesses and education.

Miami Bayside Foundation

 

Miami-Dade Beacon Council

The Miami-Dade Beacon Council, a public-private partnership, facilitates business growth and expansion locally, nationally and internationally.

Miami-Dade Beacon Council: Discover the New Business Ecosystem

 

Miami-Dade County Economic Development Office

The mission of the Miami-Dade County Economic Development office is to foster economic vitality by capitalizing on their strengths and by supporting investments in key emerging industries that increase opportunities for small businesses and the local workforce.
Economic Development

miamidade.gov

 

Miami-Dade County International Trade Consortium

The office of the Miami-Dade County International Trade Consortium (ITC) serves as the County’s lead liaison to foreign governments and works closely with other offices to ensure protocol procedures and services are being met.
Miami-Dade County International Trade Consortium

miamidade.gov

 

Miami-Dade County Small Business Development Office

SBD is a unit of the Office of the Mayor and is responsible for the administration of Miami-Dade County's Small Business Enterprise, Wage and Workforce Programs.
Small Business Development

miamidade.gov

 

Miami Business Development Agency (MBDA)

MBDA’s programs offer customized business development and industry-focused services to provide greater access to capital, contracts and markets.
Minority Business Development Agency

mbda.gov

 

Prospera

Prospera is an economic development, nonprofit organization specialized in providing bilingual assistance to Hispanic entrepreneurs trying to establish or expand their business.
Prospera Florida - Helping Hispanic Entrepreneurs Grow

prosperausa.org

 

Select Florida, Inc.

Select Florida serves as the state’s primary entity for trade and export development supporting more than 60,000 Florida exporting businesses

Grow Your Business in Florida | SelectFlorida

 

Startup FIU

StartUP FIU is a university-wide innovation hub that fosters and develops entrepreneurship and innovation to help our students, researchers, and community connect, contribute to, and thrive in today’s fast changing world.

StartUP FIU

 

The Idea Center at Miami-Dade College

The Idea Center can help launch or scale your business, nonprofit, or social enterprise through one on one coaching, access to capital, connecting you with mentors, and much more.

The Idea Center

 

U.S. Department of Commerce

The Department of Commerce’s mission is to create the conditions for economic growth and opportunity for all communities.

U.S. Department of Commerce

 

U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA)

The U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA) delivers loans, loan guarantees, contracts, counseling sessions and other forms of assistance to small businesses.
Small Business Administration

sba.gov
