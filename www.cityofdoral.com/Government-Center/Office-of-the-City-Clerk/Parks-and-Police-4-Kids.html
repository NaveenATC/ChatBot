Parks and Police 4 Kids

About

Follow Us on Social Media
Facebook
Instagram

Park & Police 4 Kids, Inc. (PP4K) is a City of Doral 501(c)(3) foundation whose mission aims to empower youth in reaching their full potential by building healthy relationships and preparing them with the necessary tools to confront and overcome challenges.

PP4K works to provide programs focused on:

  • Mentoring our youth
  • Educational initiatives for youth sports
  • Children's health care
  • Recreational after school programs


The Parks and Police 4 Kids Foundation Board Members are:

Mayor Christi Fraga, Chair
Chief Edwin Lopez, Vice Chair
Councilwoman Nicole Reinoso, Board Member
Michael Festa, Parks and Recreation Director, Treasurer
Josh Rios, Secretary

Funding Policy and Funding Application

This policy sets guidelines for the disbursement of funds by PARKS & POLICE 4 KIDS, INC. ("PP4K") to community projects within the City of Doral that align with the organization's mission of facilitating recreation, education, and social programs for young residents, with a focus on preventing juvenile misconduct.

This policy aims to create a positive and safe environment for Doral's youth, enhancing the community as a whole. For more information, below is the policy as well as the application.

Traffic Safety Comic Book

Regina Gallardo curated this series of comics with the goal of educating children on the importance of following the rules that are designed to keep a community safe. Regina moved to Doral in 2012 from Mexico City and has since then been involved in Doral’s Girl Scout Troop 552. This project is part of her social service project for her Gold Award which is the highest achievement a Girl Scout can get. Regina attends the School for Advanced Studies at the Miami Dade College West Campus.
