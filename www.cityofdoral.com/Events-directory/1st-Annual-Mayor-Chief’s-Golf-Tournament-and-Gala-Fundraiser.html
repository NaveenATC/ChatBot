1st Annual - Mayor & Chief’s Golf Tournament and Gala Fundraiser

Registration Closed

Two Events. One Mission. A First for Doral.

Thank you to our community, partners, and generous sponsors for making the inaugural Mayor & Chief’s Golf Tournament and Gala possible. Because of your support, the Parks & Police 4 Kids Foundation (PP4K) can expand mentorship, academic support, sports, and arts programs that uplift Doral’s youth.

Give Back Today

Couldn’t attend but still want to make a difference? Scan the QR code on this page to donate now and help us continue serving more families through PP4K.

 

With Gratitude to Our Sponsors

We extend our sincere appreciation to all sponsors who joined us to support PP4K. Your commitment strengthens our community and creates opportunities for our kids every day.

Relive the fun!
