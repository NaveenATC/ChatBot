Freebee in Doral

Feebee Program

  • Freebee is a FREE, door-to-door, on-demand transportation service, brought to you by the City of Doral!
  • Freebee vehicles are 100% electric, and emission-free!
  • Your Freebee guide will share with you the latest and greatest happenings in the City of Doral.
  • Freebee caters to any and all ages, from children to seniors, and all types of pets.
  • Freebee provides booster seats as appropriate for children who exceed the size requirements to travel in a car seat.
  • Smaller children and infants are required to be secured in a federally approved car seat, provided by their parent or guardian.
  • Freebee will direct you to a safe pickup location and will not stop in the middle of traffic.

Freebee for Seniors

Doral Launches Freebee Program for Senior Citizens!

In a move to prioritize the well-being of its senior citizens, the City of Doral is proud to announce the launch of a new transportation initiative in partnership with Freebee. This innovative pilot program offers free, door-to-door transportation for residents aged 65 and above, aiming to improve mobility and access to essential services.

Accessibility and Community Engagement

 The Freebee program is a significant step toward increasing accessibility for Doral's senior citizens. By providing reliable transportation to essential locations such as shopping centers, parks, and healthcare facilities, the program enhances the quality of life for older residents and helps them remain active and connected within the community.

How the Program Works

 Seniors can easily request a ride through Freebee's dispatch line or mobile app. After registering and providing a valid ID during their first ride, residents can enjoy the service without needing to present their ID again. This streamlined process ensures quick and efficient transportation for senior citizens. Residents can request rides by calling Freebee’s dispatch line at (855) 918-3733 or through the Ride Freebee mobile app. Available on iOS and Android.

Eligibility

 To register for the program, residents must be aged 65 or older and call the Freebee dispatch line to create a profile.

Service Hours:

 Monday – Thursday: 8:00 am – 4:00 pm
Friday: 8:00 am – 8:00 pm
Sunday: 9:00 am – 3:00 pm

Service Area

 Freebee provides rides within Doral city limits to a range of points of interest, including parks, supermarkets, pharmacies, medical facilities, and more.
