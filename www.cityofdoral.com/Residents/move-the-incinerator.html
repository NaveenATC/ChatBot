Help us move the Incinerator out of Doral

July 17th

A pivotal decision was reached today, July 16, 2025, during the Miami-Dade County Board of County Commissioners Meeting. Commissioners voted to approve the exploration of additional sites for the County’s waste-to-energy facility, with a definitive stipulation: certain cities, including the City of Doral, will not be considered for its future location. This decision follows years of Doral enduring the impacts of the existing waste-to-energy facility within its city limits. This decision is contingent on Doral negotiating associated costs with the County. The City of Doral will work to ensure financial terms are fair and just, protecting residents' quality of life.

In a statement following the vote, Mayor Christi Fraga acknowledged the significance: "Today marks a defining moment for the City of Doral... This is the outcome we have fought for—one that reflects the will of our residents, the power of our unity, and the importance of standing up for what’s right."

Our City remains vigilant until negotiations are finalized and our community's well-being is secured. We strongly encourage all residents to stay engaged and vocal throughout this ongoing process to definitively safeguard our shared well-being and quality of life.

¡Doral Ya No Será Considerado como Futuro Sitio para la Planta de Conversión de Residuos en Energía!

Una decisión trascendental se alcanzó hoy, 16 de julio de 2025, durante la Reunión de la Junta de Comisionados del Condado de Miami-Dade. Los comisionados votaron para aprobar la exploración de sitios adicionales para la planta de conversión de residuos en energía del Condado, con una estipulación definitiva: ciertas ciudades, incluyendo la Ciudad de Doral, no serán consideradas para su futura ubicación. Esta decisión se da después de años en que Doral ha soportado los impactos de la planta existente dentro de los límites de la ciudad.

Esta exclusión está condicionada a que Doral negocie los costos asociados con el Condado. La Ciudad de Doral trabajará para asegurar que los términos financieros sean justos y equitativos, protegiendo la calidad de vida de los residentes.

En un comunicado posterior a la votación, la Alcaldesa Christi Fraga reconoció la importancia de este momento: "Hoy marca un momento decisivo para la Ciudad de Doral... Este es el resultado por el que hemos luchado, uno que refleja la voluntad de nuestros residentes, el poder de nuestra unidad y la importancia de defender lo que es correcto."

Nuestra Ciudad se mantiene vigilante hasta que las negociaciones finalicen y el bienestar de nuestra comunidad esté asegurado. Instamos encarecidamente a todos los residentes a mantenerse comprometidos y vocalizar durante todo este proceso continuo para salvaguardar definitivamente nuestro bienestar y calidad de vida compartidos.

July 14th

The Miami-Dade County Board of Commissioners is scheduled to revisit the future location of the proposed waste-to-energy facility at its upcoming meeting on Wednesday, July 16, 2025.

County Mayor Daniella Levine Cava’s recommendation—originally presented in January 2025—remains unchanged: to continue long-haul waste transportation via truck and rail, without rebuilding the facility in Doral, while exploring the possibility of developing a new landfill site outside of Miami-Dade County. A final vote on this matter is expected at the meeting.

When: Wednesday, July 16, 2025
Time: Meeting starts at 9:00 a.m.
Where: Stephen P. Clark Government Center, 111 NW 1st Street, 27th Floor, Miami, FL 33128

We encourage community members to voice your feedback by contacting the Miami Dade County Mayor and Board of Commissioners. Stay engaged. Stay vocal. Together, we can protect the future of Doral.

March 7th

The Miami-Dade County Board of Commissioners has once again postponed a decision on the future location of a proposed waste incinerator. Instead of selecting a site, commissioners agreed to revisit the issue on July 16, 2025, after receiving a comprehensive report exploring all available options for managing solid waste.

The incinerator discussion, which has been ongoing for two years, was initially expected to conclude at the February 19 meeting following a workshop held on January 28. However, commissioners deferred item 11A6, which proposed excluding Doral, Medley, and NW 58th Street as potential locations, and instead directed the administration to prepare a detailed report analyzing alternatives, including landfilling and waste-to-energy technologies.

The report, due within 120 days, will examine financial, environmental, and logistical considerations related to Miami-Dade’s waste management system. Commissioners emphasized the need for a thorough analysis to make an informed decision. The issue is set to be discussed again on July 16, 2025, at which point a final decision on waste management options is anticipated.

Stay informed on this developing story by following our social media channels and visiting our website for the latest updates. Remember, change in Doral happens when you use your voice. Stay informed, stay proactive, and together, we can keep our city clean and safe!

The City of Doral Asks for Resident Involvement at the December 3rd Miami-Dade County Commission Meeting Regarding the Waste to Energy Incinerator

November 25, 2024

Doral, FL – For years, the City of Doral’s leadership and residents have come together, imploring Miami-Dade County to relocate the incinerator outside of our community where it would no longer cause detrimental wellness, health and quality of life affects to our families.

During a special meeting held on November 4th, the City Council unanimously instructed the City’s administration and attorneys to take all action necessary to prohibit the relocation of the incinerator in Doral, and provide a good faith offer of $40 million for its relocation.

“We are disappointed to learn of the newly released report by Miami-Dade County Mayor Daniella Levine Cava where she recommends that the new incinerator be rebuilt in Doral,” stated Mayor Christi Fraga. “We will continue to fight for what is right and fair for our community.”

The City’s leadership remains committed to doing whatever possible to work with Miami-Dade County to see that the incinerator is moved to a more suitable location where so many families are not affected, and a location that provides a long-term, sustainable solution for all County residents.

Now is not the time to give up! We must remain united, active, and engaged as a community, fighting for this important and common goal. We ask that the community take part in the discussion on Tuesday, December 3rd, as the Miami-Dade County’s Board of Commissioners will discuss the facility and ultimately make a decision on its future location. This meeting is open to the public and you are invited to attend, as the City of Doral’s leadership will also be present in their continued efforts to have the incinerator moved outside of our community where it would no longer harm the health and quality of life of our residents.

The City will be providing two buses for transport from Doral Government Center (8401 NW 53rd Terrace) to Miami-Dade County’s Stephen P. Clark Government Center (first come, first serve). Residents are asked to arrive at the Doral Government Center by 6:30 a.m. on December 3rd – buses will depart promptly at 7:00 a.m. Free parking will be available in the Doral Government Center parking garage.

Additional Transport Options

  • Via Palmetto Metrorail Station → Stephen P. Clark Government Center
    Station Address: 7701 NW 79 Avenue
    Connecting Metrobus Service:
    87, Doral Route 2, Doral Route 3

  • Doral Trolley Stop Number 2060 → Palmetto Metrorail Station → Stephen P. Clark Government Center
    Via Route 2 from Downtown Doral (53rd street stop)
    Estimated departure time from Doral Trolley stop is 7:22 a.m.

We also encourage you to voice your feedback by contacting the Miami Dade County Mayor and Board of Commissioners.
